Quick start

  1. Open the Studio. Pick a template (House, Shrine, Temple, Cloister, Rotunda, Bridge or Hall) from File ▸ Start from a template, or start empty.
  2. Open the Library tab, search or browse, and click an item to add it at the centre of the view. Tick Click-to-place mode to stamp copies by clicking.
  3. Select an object and use the gizmo to move it, or open Edit to type exact values and change its parameters and material.
  4. Your work is auto-saved in this browser. Use File ▸ Save to keep a .arck.json file you can reopen anywhere.

1 unit = 1 metre. Everything sits on the ground plane (y = 0).

Library

The Library holds the same parametric catalog as the Shapes, Structures and Components pages — about 150 items. Use the search box or the category chips. Click a card to add the item at the centre of the view, or tick Click-to-place mode to stamp repeated copies by clicking in the scene (R rotates 90°, Esc stops). On desktop you can also drag a card onto the canvas.

Open any item's page for a live 3D preview, parameter sliders, materials, section view, a human-scale figure and downloads, plus a short "how to use it" note.

Walls, rooms & slabs

Wall W

  1. Click to set the start, click again for each corner. The length label follows the cursor.
  2. Type a number (metres) then Enter to place a point exactly that far in the cursor's direction.
  3. Enter, Finish or double-clicking the last point ends the run; Close joins back to the start; Undo point removes the last segment; Esc cancels.

The options bar sets wall height, thickness, material and level, plus grid snap and Ortho (locks to 90°). Walls snap to the grid and to existing wall ends (green marker).

Room Q

Click two opposite corners: you get four walls, a floor slab, a door and windows. Edit each wall afterwards in the Edit tab.

Slab F

Click the outline of any polygon floor or roof deck; click near the first point or press Enter to close it.

Move · rotate · scale

V select, T move, R rotate, S scale. Drag the coloured arrows or rings of the gizmo. Hold Shift and click to multi-select (on a phone toggle Multi-select in the options bar), or drag nothing and press Ctrl+A for everything. Arrow keys nudge by the snap step; Shift nudges 4×; PageUp/PageDown lifts and lowers.

Multiple objects share a pivot. Align, Distribute, Mirror, Array (linear or around a point), Group and To ground are in the Edit tab.

Editing & materials

The Edit tab shows the selected object's name, exact position / rotation / scale (type values and press Tab), the item's parameters (the same sliders as on the catalog page) and a material picker — stone, brick, timber, marble, gold, glass, concrete, plaster and more. Changes can be undone with Ctrl+Z. Lock an object (padlock in the Scene tab) to stop accidental moves.

Scene & layers

The Scene tab is an outliner: click to select, the eye hides and the padlock locks objects; objects belong to layers (assign an object's layer in the Edit tab). Hide a layer to declutter the view.

View, section & sun

  • Camera views: 1 iso, 2 top, 3 front, 4 right, 5 back, 6 left, Home fit all, . frame selection, O perspective/orthographic.
  • Render modes: Solid, Clay (form study), Wire, X-ray.
  • Section cut: turn on, pick X / Y / Z, slide the plane and flip it to see inside buildings — then take a picture.
  • Sun & shadow study: set latitude, longitude, date and hour; the sun and shadows follow real solar positions. Try 21 June vs 21 December.
  • Themes: dark, light and sky backgrounds. Picture saves a PNG at 1× or 2×.

Measuring & notes

M Measure: click two points — they snap to corners and vertices — to leave a dimension line. N adds a text note anchored in 3D. L adds a light you can move; spot lights make good "theatre" renders.

Plan & quantities

The Plan tab lists every material in the scene with volume and area, plus a cost estimate using editable rates, and exports a CSV schedule for a spreadsheet. Costs are learning estimates, not quotations.

On a phone or tablet

Below 900 px wide the Studio switches to a touch layout: the viewport fills the screen, a bottom bar opens Library / Edit / Scene / View / Plan as a sheet you can drag up, a tool strip sits above it, and a floating bar under the selection offers Move, Rotate, Scale, Copy, Edit and Delete.

  • One finger: orbit · Two fingers: pan and zoom · Tap: select · Tap empty space: deselect
  • Walls: tap points; type lengths in the field in the options bar.
  • Use landscape on tablets for the desktop-style side panel (≥ 900 px).

Add the site to your home screen for a full-screen app that works offline once loaded.

Catalog pages

Shapes (solids, polyhedra, curved forms, plan shapes), Structures (arches, domes, vaults, trusses, bridges, towers, monuments, frames, footings) and Components (walls, doors, windows, columns, roofs, stairs, furniture, fixtures, landscape). Click a tile for the detail view: orbit it, change sliders, swap materials, cut a section, switch to wireframe, show a human-scale figure and download GLB / OBJ / STL — or Add to Studio. Links like components.html#column-doric open an item directly.

Viewer

Drop any supported file on the Viewer. You get render modes, a section cut, a two-click measure, scale fixes for unit mix-ups, a parts list with visibility toggles, picture and format export, and Open in Studio to keep building around it.

Calculators

Tools has stairs (Blondel's rule), roof pitch, ramps, segmental arch geometry, area & volume, concrete, bricks, paint, a simple beam check, sun angle, unit converter and a rough cost estimate. Many have a 3D preview and an Open in Studio button. Results are for learning and early design, not for construction.

FAQ

Is my work uploaded anywhere?
No. ARCK is a static site. Auto-save uses your browser's IndexedDB; everything else stays in files you save.

My model is invisible or tiny
It is probably in millimetres or centimetres. Use the unit hint in the Studio Edit tab or Viewer's Scale buttons, then press Home to fit.

Textures are missing on my OBJ
Select the .obj, the .mtl and the image files together when importing. Browsers cannot read neighbouring files by themselves.

It feels slow on my phone
Switch to Clay or Wire mode, turn shadows off in the View tab, and avoid importing meshes above ~500 k triangles.

Can I get my project into Blender, SketchUp or Revit?
Export GLB (or OBJ) from File ▸ Export. Blender opens GLB directly; SketchUp and Revit usually need an import plugin or an OBJ/FBX bridge.
